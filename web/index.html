Privacy

Your files stay on your PC.

Online converters need you to upload a copy of your file. That puts private documents, photos, and metadata on a server you do not control.

↑

Uploading adds risk

Your file leaves your PC, so you have to trust how another service stores, processes, and deletes it.

⌂

Clementine works locally

Conversions and file tools run on your PC. Your files are never uploaded to us.

Tap Shift on your keyboard

⇧shift
⇧ Convert⎇ Tools⌃ Actions

Drag a file, then tap Shift for conversions, Alt for file tools, or Control for actions. Release the key and keep dragging; the wheel stays open until you drop or cancel. Or press Enter on a file to open the format menu, then use Alt or Control to switch wheels. Use arrow keys to choose, Enter to apply, and Escape to cancel. On a touch screen, drag to move or hold a file briefly before dragging to convert. All operations on this page are simulated.
